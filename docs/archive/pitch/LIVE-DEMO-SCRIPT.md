## PRESENTER NOTES

### Energy & Pacing
- Speak with enthusiasm but not salesperson energy
- Pause after key stats ("$280 million," "87% confidence")
- Make eye contact with different stakeholders
- Use farmer language, not tech jargon

### Handling Skepticism
- Acknowledge limitations ("This doesn't replace extension agents")
- Show confidence scores (transparency builds trust)
- Cite LSU research specifically (you know their work)
- Invite critique ("What would make this more useful for your farmers?")

### Reading the Room
- If they're excited about research data → emphasize Model 1 partnership
- If they're worried about budget → emphasize grant funding opportunities
- If they're protective of farmers → emphasize data privacy and ownership
- If they're skeptical of AI → show LSU-specific training and source citations

### Closing Strong
- Don't oversell—LSU AgCenter doesn't respond to hype
- End with a clear ask (30-day follow-up)
- Offer to leave demo device for them to test
- Thank them for their research (you're building on their work)
